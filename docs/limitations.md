# VeriData Limitations & Scope (`docs/limitations.md`)

1. **Observational Association Only:** VeriData evaluates bivariate statistical associations (`numeric_vs_binary`, `numeric_vs_categorical`, `numeric_vs_numeric`, `categorical_vs_categorical`). It does not infer causal direction, adjust for confounders by default, or establish causality.
2. **Row Independence Assumption:** All built-in tests (Welch *t*, Mann-Whitney *U*, Welch ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Pearson, Spearman, chi-square, Fisher's exact) assume independent observations. Longitudinal, repeated-measures, spatial, or hierarchically clustered rows can inflate Type-I error.
3. **BH Dependence & Borderline `q`-Values:** Benjamini-Hochberg (1995) controls FDR under independence or PRDS positive dependence across tests. Because pairwise tests share columns, arbitrary negative dependencies can occur; `--correction by` (Benjamini-Yekutieli) is provided as a conservative alternative. Additionally, BH bounds the *average* false share across the accepted family, so findings with `q` close to `0.05` have a higher empirical false fraction than `q < 0.001` discoveries.
4. **Effect-Size Floor Power Trade-Off:** Filtering accepted discoveries by Cohen's "small" effect floor after BH prevents practically trivial findings at large `n` without inflating FDP in expectation, but sacrifices recall for small effects in small samples (`n <= 100`).
5. **Default Linearity / Monotonicity Scope:** Nonlinear non-monotonic patterns (e.g., U-shaped quadratics) and higher-order interactions (`A x B -> C`) are excluded from default exhaustive testing (`hypotheses.enable_nonlinear: false`).
6. **Nominal Post-Selection Confidence Intervals:** Stage B 95% confidence intervals are unadjusted for post-selection multiplicity and should be interpreted as descriptive precision around the observed sample effect.
7. **Deterministic Derived Columns:** Near-deterministic pairs (`|r| >= 0.999`, `V_bc >= 0.99`) are flagged and rejected as `trivial_relationship` to suppress duplicate/derived features, which may also suppress genuine near-identity physical measurements.
8. **Public-Dataset Memorization in LLM Baselines:** When benchmarking LLM-only baselines on public Kaggle/UCI datasets (`E5`), models may recall dataset structure from pretraining; therefore, the primary FDR/recall claim rests on contamination-free synthetic planted-truth suites (`E1`–`E2`) and permuted-null/split-half checks.
